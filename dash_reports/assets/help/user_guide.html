📊 Understanding the Response

When you ask a question, the bot organizes its response into collapsible sections:

Common Sections You'll See:

INPUTS & ASSUMPTIONS 🔽
Shows what parameters and assumptions the bot used for your query
The Confidence Score appears here at the top
DATA RESULTS 🔽
Contains the table with your query results (always expanded by default)
KEY INSIGHTS 🔽
Highlights important takeaways from the data (always expanded by default)
ISSUES & CLARIFICATIONS ▶️
Shows any warnings or clarifications needed (collapsed by default)

Confidence Score:

Located in the INPUTS & ASSUMPTIONS section

✅ Confidence: 95% - High confidence (≥80%)
The bot is confident the SQL query correctly follows best practices and schema rules.

⚠️ Confidence: 65% - Lower confidence (<80%)
The query may have ambiguities or potential issues. Review the results carefully.
The confidence score is based on how well the generated SQL follows the system's instructions, including schema adherence, filter logic, and query clarity.

Table Format:

Results are displayed in a clean, formatted table with:

  • Bold headers for easy identification
  • Alternating row colors for readability
  • Proper number formatting (currency, percentages)
  • Emoji indicators for rankings (🥇🥈🥉)
Example: "What is the beta history for CSTRAT?" showing formatted table with data

Key Insights:

The bot automatically generates 2-3 key takeaways from your data, helping you quickly understand the most important findings.

🔵 Understanding the Blue Arrows

Blue arrows mean collapsible sections - click them to expand or collapse different parts of the response.

What are the Blue Arrows?

The 🔽 blue arrows in the bot's responses are collapsible section toggles. They allow you to expand or collapse different parts of the response for better readability.

Example: "What is the beta for CSTRAT?" showing collapsible sections with blue arrows

How to Use Collapsible Sections:

Step 1: Look for sections with blue arrows (🔽 or ▶️)
Step 2: Click on the section header to expand/collapse
Step 3: Expanded sections show 🔽, collapsed sections show ▶️
The most important sections (DATA RESULTS and KEY INSIGHTS) are expanded by default so you can see your data immediately!

🦴 Giving Treats

Help your Golden Retriever intern learn and grow! Just like training a good pup, your feedback helps improve performance.

How to Give Treats:

Your pup recognizes treats automatically!
Just chat naturally - when you say things like "good job!", "this looks great!", or "not quite right", your Golden Retriever intern picks up on it and learns from your feedback.

Types of Treats:

🦴 Good Pup Treats
"This is exactly what I needed!", "Perfect!", "Great results!", "Good job!"
🎾 Playful Nudges
"This doesn't look right", "Not what I expected", "Can you try again?"
🐕 Training Tips
"It would be helpful if...", "Could you also show...", "I'd like to see..."
No special commands needed - just respond naturally and your feedback helps train the intern!

⌨️ Available Commands

Quick reference for bot commands:

help or /help
Show the help message with all available commands and example queries
health or /health
Check the system health status and verify the bot is working correctly
tables or /tables
List all available database tables you can query

Clear your conversation history and start fresh

💡 Tips & Best Practices

Get the most out of the APO Risk Intern:

Be specific with your questions - use exact fund codes like "CSTRAT" instead of ambiguous terms like "strategy fund" or "credit strategy"
Use clear time references (e.g., "as of today", "latest", "current")
If you get unexpected results, try rephrasing your question or use the "clear" command to start fresh
Click on the blue arrows (🔽) to expand/collapse sections and focus on what matters most
Large result sets truncated - automatically limited to 50 rows for performance. If you need more data, ask for specific filters

❓ Getting Help & Troubleshooting

Getting Help:

Method 1: Using the Help Command

  1. Type help in the chat
  2. Hit Send ➤
  3. You'll see a list of available commands and example queries
Help command showing available commands and example queries

Method 2: Using View Prompts Menu

  1. Click on the View Prompts button (📋 icon) at the bottom of the chat
  2. Select Help from the menu
  3. Click Send ➤
  4. The bot will display all available commands and examples
View Prompts menu showing Help, Health, and Tables options

Troubleshooting:

Bot not responding?
• Check system health with health command
• Clear conversation history with clear
• Rephrase your question more simply
Unexpected results?
• Check INPUTS & ASSUMPTIONS section
• Review the confidence score
• Be more specific with fund codes, dates, or metrics
• Give feedback by saying "doesn't look right", "incorrect", or "not good" - the bot will log it
